Our Story

Our roastery was founded on the idea of community collaboration and the environmentally conscious sourcing and brewing of coffee. To emphasize this, we chose to locate our roastery right next to a botanical garden, providing increased foot traffic and visitations to both of our organizations.

Our Mission

Since its opening, our roastery has made a commitment to foster a community of environmentally minded people in collaboration with the Botanical Garden. To accomplish this, the roastery hosts monthly events to boost collaboration, some in collaboration with the Botanical Garden.

Our Partners

We partner with Hacienda Iluminada, a family-owned coffee bean farm based in Maricao, Puerto Rico. They utilize a mixture of both traditional and modern growing techniques to provide high-quality coffee beans, with an emphasis on ecological and sustainable practices.
